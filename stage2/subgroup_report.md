## Verdict

Criterion (fixed before results): an image signal is useful if it raises field-edit recall by at least 20 points.

- Tamper CNN: field-edit recall 0.081 -> 0.097 (+0.016); raw AUC on field edits 0.60. Does NOT meet the 20-point criterion.
- Noise forensics: field-edit recall 0.097 -> 0.548 (+0.452). Meets the 20-point criterion.

## Caveat

Synthetic data from generate_synthetic_claims.py; the forgeries are generated by the same project that designed the detectors.
